# Privacy policy

Roam does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The app uses your device's GPS through the location permission for a single purpose:
calculating the speed shown on screen. Location data never leaves your device. The app
has no network access, no analytics, no advertising and no third party services of any
kind.

Settings (units, brightness, display options) are stored locally on your device and
nowhere else.
